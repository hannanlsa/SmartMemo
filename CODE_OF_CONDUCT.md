# Code of Conduct

> **All participants in the SmartMemo project — maintainers, contributors,
> issue reporters, and community members — are expected to follow this
> Code of Conduct.**

## 1. Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in the SmartMemo project a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of:

- Age
- Body size
- Disability
- Ethnicity
- Sex characteristics
- Gender identity and expression
- Level of experience
- Nationality
- Personal appearance
- Race
- Religion (or lack thereof)
- Sexual identity and orientation

## 2. Our Standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the project
- Showing empathy towards other community members
- Refraining from discussing how to use SmartMemo to attack, evade,
  reverse-engineer, or specifically target any named commercial
  product
- Refraining from solicitation of paid services that would specifically
  weaponise SmartMemo against a named commercial product

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- The use of sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual
  attention or advances
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political
  attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or
  electronic address, without explicit permission
- Discussing the use of SmartMemo to record, intercept, or evade the
  privacy controls of any specific named commercial product or
  individual
- Soliciting, offering, or arranging paid "customisation" services
  that would target a specific commercial product
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in
  a professional setting

## 3. Project-Specific Off-Topic List

The following topics are **off-topic** in every public channel of this
project (issues, discussions, chat, social media, etc.):

1. **"How do I use SmartMemo against Product X?"** — generic technical
   question. We can answer "SmartMemo is a generic tool; configure it
   as you see fit, subject to your local law." We will not provide
   step-by-step instructions targeting a specific product.
2. **"Can you add a preset for Product X?"** — out of scope. The
   project deliberately ships with an empty configuration.
3. **"How do I bypass Product X's audio capture opt-out?"** — out of
   scope and a violation of `CONTRIBUTING.md` section 4.2.
4. **"I am being harassed by Product X; can SmartMemo help me
   capture evidence?"** — out of scope. We do not give legal advice.
   Consult a licensed attorney in your jurisdiction.
5. **Selling, reselling, or "official" support contracts for SmartMemo**
   as a service that specifically targets a named commercial product —
   prohibited under `CONTRIBUTING.md` section 4.4.
6. **DMCA-style threats or "I will sue you" messages** — please use
   the responsible-disclosure channel in `SECURITY.md`. Threats in
   public channels will be removed and may be reported.

## 4. Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour
may be reported by contacting the project maintainer at the address
listed in `SECURITY.md`. All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated and will result in a response that is deemed necessary
and appropriate to the circumstances.

The maintainer is obligated to maintain confidentiality with regard
to the reporter of an incident. Further details of specific
enforcement policies may be posted separately.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce the Code of Conduct in good
faith may face temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by
other members of the project's leadership.
